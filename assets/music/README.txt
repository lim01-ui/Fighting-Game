Background music
================

intro_title.mp3 is the "title screen" track.
fight_loop.mp3 is the "level1" track, described by the uploader as one of
five seamlessly looping chiptunes.

Both tracks are by SubspaceAudio, from "5 Chiptunes (Action)" on
OpenGameArt.org:
https://opengameart.org/content/5-chiptunes-action

The OpenGameArt asset page lists the upload under the Creative Commons
CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 does not require attribution. Credit is included here as a courtesy.
