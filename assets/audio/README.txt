Game sound effects
==================

Every sound effect used by the game is sourced from the following OpenGameArt
assets, each of which is listed as CC0 1.0 Universal on its source page.
The intro and fight music are separate assets documented in ../music/README.txt.

Menu highlight and select:
  Author: Fupi
  https://opengameart.org/content/8bit-menu-highlight
  https://opengameart.org/content/8bit-menu-select

Menu back and round/victory cue:
  Author: Sudocolon
  https://opengameart.org/content/menu-rollover
  https://opengameart.org/content/menu-choice

Punch, impact, block, and knockout sounds:
  Collection: 37 hits/punches
  Author/source credited on OpenGameArt: Independent.nu, submitted by qubodup
  https://opengameart.org/content/37-hitspunches

Attack, projectile, and movement swishes:
  Author: qubodup
  https://opengameart.org/content/swish-bamboo-stick-weapon-swhoshes

The audio samples are converted to PCM WAV where appropriate for reliable
playback across supported Pygame/SDL_mixer builds. CC0 does not require
attribution; source details are retained here as a courtesy and provenance
record.
