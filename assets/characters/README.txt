Character asset sources and licenses
====================================

Renegade
--------
Asset: Brawler Asset Character "Vigilante" SMS (free Renegade files)
Author: Chasersgaming
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/brawler-asset-character-vigilante-sms
License: CC0 1.0 Universal

Soldier
-------
Asset: RPG Asset Character "Soldier" SMS
Author: Chasersgaming
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-asset-character-soldier-sms
License: CC0 1.0 Universal

Both sprite sets are distributed under CC0 on their OpenGameArt pages and are
included unchanged in their respective folders.

Saikyo is a separate CC0 pixel-art fighter; its source and license are
documented in saikyo/README.txt.

Roster organization
-------------------
The original fighters are grouped together in characters/character_data.py.
The imported open-source fighters are grouped together at the end of that
roster. Each roster entry points to its own ultimate definition in ULTIMATES.
