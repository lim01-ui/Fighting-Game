Title screen video
==================

title_screen.mp4 is the user-provided 10-second game title video. The opening
from 0:00 to 0:07 plays once; playback cuts directly to 0:08 and loops the
0:08-to-0:09 segment until any keyboard, mouse, or controller button is pressed.
The game then continues to its main menu.

The MP4 contains an audio stream, but the game intentionally uses its existing
CC0 intro music instead. The embedded video audio is not played.
